Terms of Use

Applies to the BuyNow, BuyNow Partner and BuyNow Rider apps · Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. What BuyNow is

BuyNow is a marketplace. Products are sold by independent stalls. The sale is a contract between you (the customer) and the stall. BuyNow provides the apps, does not sell the goods, and does not hold or process customer or stall money.

2. Orders and the down payment

To discourage cancelled and fake orders, a stall may ask for a small down payment (a “trust charge”). It is calculated per stall on the product subtotal, without the delivery fee:

Product subtotal (per stall)Down payment
Under ৳50020% (at least ৳20)
৳500 to ৳2,000৳100
Over ৳2,00010% (rounded up)
  • The minimum order is ৳100 per stall.
  • After you place the order, send the down payment to the stall's bKash or Nagad number shown in your order, then enter the transaction ID (TrxID) in the app. The money goes directly to the stall.
  • The stall confirms that it received the payment. After that your order is confirmed.
  • You pay the rest in cash when the order is delivered.
  • Some stalls may not ask for a down payment.

4. Stalls (BuyNow Partner)

  • BuyNow charges no commission on sales and currently no subscription fee. If that changes we will update these terms and tell stall owners first. The apps contain no donation and no in-app purchase.
  • Your stall is visible to customers only while it is verified and active, or while it is a live trial stall.
  • We may hide or freeze a stall for complaints, suspected fraud or false information.
  • You must keep your bKash/Nagad number correct, confirm or reject down payments promptly, and refund them when these terms say so.
  • Customer details you receive may be used only to fulfil and support that order.

4a. Cattle Market (cattle advertisements)

  • An advertisement must describe a real animal that you own and may sell. Remove it or mark it sold when the animal is sold.
  • The sale is between the buyer and the seller. BuyNow does not hold money and does not guarantee the animal, its health or its weight.
  • The exact place of a farm is shared only when its owner approves a visitor's request. An owner may decline or block a visitor. Do not misuse the phone number or name you receive.

4b. Delivery riders (BuyNow Rider)

  • Riders are independent. A rider buys the goods of an open request with their own money, hands them to the customer, and receives the cash and the delivery charge as shown in the app.
  • A rider must take a job only inside their delivery area, must use the OTP and proof photo honestly, and must use customer details only for that delivery.
  • We may freeze a rider account for fraud, false documents or complaints.
