How the apps work together

When a shopper places an order in BuyNow, the stall that sells the items receives the order in BuyNow Partner, including the delivery details needed to fulfil it, and a delivery rider can take the delivery in BuyNow Rider. Each app has its own privacy policy and deletion page because the data and the people involved are different.
