BuyNow Partner Privacy Policy

BuyNow Partner · Android package com.buynow.vendor

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how BuyNow Partner (package com.buynow.vendor), published by Joynul's Software Labs, collects, uses and protects information about stall owners and about the customers whose orders you receive. By using the app you agree to it. If you are a shopper, see the BuyNow policy.

2. How we use your information

  • Create and manage your stall account
  • Verify your stall, contact you and manage your account
  • Show your stall and products to shoppers
  • Deliver orders and returns to you and notify you
  • Keep the marketplace secure, review customer reports, and hide or freeze a stall for fraud, complaints or false information
  • Meet legal and accounting obligations

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as follows:

  • Shoppers: your stall name, description, approximate location (rounded, about 1 km), products, ratings and policies are public in the BuyNow app. Your exact location is sent to one shopper only when you tap Send Location on that shopper's request, and it works for 24 hours. Your bKash and Nagad numbers are shown to customers who order from you, so they can pay the down payment. Your contact number and verification documents are not shown to shoppers.
  • Delivery riders: a rider who takes your order (for example through Call a rider) can see your exact stall location and phone for that one delivery, and the customer's name, phone and address.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging, App Check), Google Play services (Sign-In, Location, Play Integrity) and Cloudinary (storage of photos). See the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.
  • Legal reasons: if the law requires it, or to protect rights and safety.

4. Money and fees

BuyNow does not hold your sales money. Customers pay you directly (cash on delivery and, if you ask for it, a down payment to your bKash or Nagad number). BuyNow charges no commission and currently no subscription fee, and the app has no donation or in-app purchase. See the Terms of Use.

4a. Customer data you receive

Customer names, phone numbers, emails and addresses are given to you only to fulfil and support that order. Do not use them for marketing, share them, or keep them longer than you need to meet your legal duties.

5. Storage and security

Your data is stored in Google Firebase and transmitted over encrypted connections, and account-based access controls limit who can read or change it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so keep your Google account protected.

6. Anonymous trial accounts

You can try the app with an anonymous trial account. Trial accounts, and their stall profile, catalogue and photos, are deleted automatically after 30 days, or sooner if you use Delete Account & Stall Now in the app. Signing in with Google makes the account permanent.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and stall data on the BuyNow Partner account deletion page. Some financial and order records must be kept longer; that page explains which.

8. Your choices

  • Edit your stall profile, catalogue and policies in the app
  • Turn off location or notifications in your device settings or in the app
  • Ask for your account and stall data to be deleted
  • Contact us with any privacy question

9. Who can use the app

BuyNow Partner is for business use by adults. It is not directed to children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top changes when we do.
