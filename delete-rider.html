Ask us to permanently delete your BuyNow Rider account and the data linked to it.

How to request deletion

  1. Fill in the form below, or email joynulabadin095@gmail.com. Please send it from the Google email linked to your rider account.
  2. We may contact you to confirm the account is yours.
  3. We delete the account and data within 30 days of a confirmed request and email you when it is done.
Before you delete: finish or hand back open deliveries and settle any cash you hold for a stall or customer, so that nobody loses money. We may ask you to settle these first.

What we delete

  • Your account (Google sign-in link) and rider profile: name, phone, vehicle, area, HOME and live location
  • Your NID and driving licence photos
  • Your delivery proof photos that still exist
  • Your push token, notification preferences and notification inbox
  • Your name and phone on delivery records

What we may keep

  • Delivery, earning, cash and complaint records that we, the stalls or the customers must keep for dispute, accounting, tax or legal reasons, kept only as long as the law requires
  • Backups and logs, which are removed on their normal cycle (up to 30 more days)

Delete only some data

To remove only some data (for example your document photos or your HOME location) without deleting the account, email us and say which data.
