BuyNow Privacy Policy

BuyNow · Android package com.buynow.app

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how BuyNow (package com.buynow.app), published by AI Team by Joynul, collects, uses and protects your information. By using the app you agree to it. If you are a stall owner, see the BuyNow Partner policy.

1. Information we collect

DataWhy we need it
Account: name, email and profile photo from your Google account, or an anonymous guest ID if you continue as guestSign-in and account identification
Profile and delivery details: name, phone number, division, district, upazila, address lineDelivering orders and contacting you about them
Location (precise or approximate), only if you allow itShowing nearby stalls and estimating distance and delivery
Orders and activity: cart, wishlist, order history, payment method (Cash on Delivery), delivery address, contact phoneProcessing and tracking orders, showing your history
Down payment details: the transaction ID (TrxID) and the mobile number you paid from, which you enter after sending a down payment to a stallLetting the stall confirm your payment and settling disputes
Order cancellation record: how many orders you cancelled, and a temporary block on new orders if you cancel too many in a short timePreventing fake or repeated cancelled orders, which cost stalls and riders time
Problem reports: reports you send (for example a refund you did not receive), with the order and stall involvedReviewing disputes and acting on them
Reviews: ratings, comments, your display name and up to 3 photos you choose to add (only after an order from that stall was delivered)Showing product and stall reviews
Photos you pick from your gallery for a review. The app opens your gallery only when you tap Add photoShowing your review photos; they are stored with our image provider (Cloudinary)
Cattle Market (cattle advertisements): your likes, comments and follows, and your location request to a farm or stall, which carries your Google name and phone numberLetting the owner decide whether to share the exact place with you. The owner sees your name and phone number
Notification preferences and push token (a device code from Firebase Cloud Messaging)Sending the alerts you chose (order status, price drops, promotions)
Technical data: network status, crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and app usage statistics such as screens opened (Firebase Analytics), handled by GoogleSecurity, abuse prevention and stability

BuyNow does not process payments, does not hold your money and has no donation or in-app purchase. A down payment is sent by you directly to the stall's bKash or Nagad number, outside the app. We do not collect card numbers or card security codes. See the Terms of Use for how down payments and refunds work.

2. How we use your information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process, deliver and track orders
  • Show relevant stalls, products and reviews
  • Send notifications you turned on
  • Keep the app secure and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Meet legal obligations

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as follows:

  • Stall owners: when you place an order, the stall that sells the items receives the order items, your name, delivery address and contact phone so it can fulfil the order. If you pay a down payment, the stall also receives the transaction ID and the number you paid from so it can confirm the payment.
  • Farm and stall owners (Cattle Market): the exact place of a farm or stall is shown to you only if its owner approves your request, for 24 hours. The owner then sees your name and phone number, and can block you.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Analytics, App Check), Google Play services (Sign-In, Location, Play Integrity) and Cloudinary (storage of review photos). See the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.
  • Legal reasons: if the law requires it, or to protect rights and safety.
  • Public content: reviews (with their photos) and comments you post are visible to other users.

4. Storage and security

Your data is stored in Google Firebase, and cart, wishlist and order data is also cached on your device. Data travels over encrypted connections, and account-based security rules limit who can read or change your cart, wishlist and orders. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

5. Guest accounts

If you continue as a guest, an anonymous account is created. Guest accounts, and the data under them, are deleted automatically after 30 days. Signing in with Google makes the account permanent.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and data on the BuyNow account deletion page.

7. Your choices

  • Edit your profile details in the app
  • Turn off location or notifications in your device settings or in the app
  • Ask for your account and data to be deleted
  • Contact us with any privacy question

8. Children

BuyNow is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top changes when we do.
