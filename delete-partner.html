Delete your BuyNow Partner account

BuyNow Partner · Android package com.buynow.vendor

Ask us to permanently delete your BuyNow Partner account, your stall and the data linked to it.

How to request deletion

  1. Fill in the form below, or email joynulabadin095@gmail.com. Please send it from the Google email linked to your stall account.
  2. We may contact you to confirm the account is yours.
  3. We delete the account and data within 30 days of a confirmed request and email you when it is done.
Before you delete: finish or cancel open orders and returns, and refund any down payments you owe customers. We may ask you to settle these first so that nobody loses money.

Trial accounts: if you use an anonymous trial account, you can delete it and its stall right away with Delete Account & Stall Now in the app profile. Trial accounts are also deleted automatically after 30 days.

What we delete

  • Your account (Google sign-in link or trial ID)
  • Your stall profile and location
  • Your product catalogue, product photos and coupons
  • Your Pashu Haat advertisements and their photos, and the visit requests sent to you
  • Your verification (KYC) information and documents (NID and shop photos)
  • Your bKash and Nagad numbers and payment settings
  • Your notification preferences

What we may keep

  • Order and return records, including down-payment transaction details, that we must keep for dispute, accounting, tax or legal reasons, kept only as long as the law requires
  • Copies of order details that shoppers already received or that were already delivered to you, as part of completed orders
  • Backups and logs, which are removed on their normal cycle (up to 30 more days)

Delete only some data

To remove only some data (for example a product, your phone number or your location) without deleting the account, email us and say which data.
