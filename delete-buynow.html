Delete your BuyNow account

BuyNow · Android package com.buynow.app

Ask us to permanently delete your BuyNow account and the personal data linked to it.

How to request deletion

  1. Fill in the form below, or email joynulabadin095@gmail.com. Please send it from the Google email linked to your account.
  2. We may contact you to confirm the account is yours.
  3. We delete the account and data within 30 days of a confirmed request and email you when it is done.

Guest users: guest accounts are deleted automatically after 30 days, and you can delete a guest account from the Profile screen in the app.

What we delete

  • Your account (Google sign-in link or guest ID)
  • Your profile: name, phone, address and location
  • Your cart and wishlist
  • Your notification preferences
  • Order history stored under your account
  • Your reviews and review photos, and your Cattle Market comments, likes and follows (deleted or made anonymous)
  • Your location requests to farms and stalls, and your push token. Your Cattle Market comments and your reviews stay but lose your name

What we may keep

  • Order records and down-payment transaction details that we or the stall owner must keep for accounting, tax, dispute or legal reasons, kept only as long as the law requires
  • Order details already delivered to stall owners to fulfil an order
  • Backups and logs, which are removed on their normal cycle (up to 30 more days)

Delete only some data

To remove only some data (for example your location or phone number) without deleting the account, email us and say which data.
