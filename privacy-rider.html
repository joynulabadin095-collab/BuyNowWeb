BuyNow Rider Privacy Policy

BuyNow Rider · Android package com.buynow.rider

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how BuyNow Rider (package com.buynow.rider), published by Joynul's Software Labs, collects, uses and protects information about delivery riders and about the customers and stalls whose orders you deliver. By using the app you agree to it. Shoppers: see the BuyNow policy. Stall owners: see the BuyNow Partner policy.

1. Information we collect

DataWhy we need it
Account: name, email and profile photo from your Google accountSign-in and account identification
Rider profile: name, phone number, vehicle, division, district and upazilaLetting stalls and customers know who is delivering, and routing jobs
Identity documents: photos of your NID and your driving licence (no numbers are typed in). They are stored privately and only BuyNow administrators can open themChecking who you are and keeping the marketplace safe
HOME location: the place you choose as your home base (it can be changed once in 30 days)Showing you only the jobs inside your delivery area
Live location (precise), only while the app is open and you are online and have allowed it. It is saved at most once every 3 minutesFinding the nearest rider for a new request, and checking that you are near the stall when you accept a job
Delivery records: orders you accept, status, times, earnings, cash you paid or received, ratings you receiveRunning the delivery, settling correctly and handling disputes
Proof of delivery: the one-time code (OTP) check, an optional photo you choose from your gallery, and your GPS position at that momentProving the parcel was handed over. The photo is deleted after 7 days
Customer and stall details for your jobs: customer name, phone and delivery address; the stall's exact place for that one deliveryDelivering the order
Notification preferences and push token (a device code from Firebase Cloud Messaging)Alerting you to new requests and updates
Technical data: network status, crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and app usage statistics (Firebase Analytics), handled by GoogleSecurity, abuse prevention and stability

The app asks to use your location or photo gallery only when a feature needs it. BuyNow does not process payments, has no donation or in-app purchase, and does not collect card numbers.

2. How we use your information

  • Create and manage your rider account and check your identity documents
  • Offer you delivery requests near you and record your deliveries and earnings
  • Prove deliveries and handle complaints and refund disputes
  • Send you notifications
  • Keep the marketplace secure, and freeze an account for fraud, false documents or complaints
  • Meet legal and accounting obligations

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as follows:

  • Stalls: the stall of an order you take sees your name, phone number and vehicle.
  • Customers: the customer of your delivery sees your name and vehicle, and may rate you.
  • BuyNow administrators and support staff: can see your profile, documents, delivery records and proof photos to check accounts and handle complaints.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Analytics, App Check), Google Play services (Sign-In, Location, Play Integrity) and Cloudinary (storage of photos).
  • Legal reasons: if the law requires it, or to protect rights and safety.

4. Storage and security

Your data is stored in Google Firebase and Cloudinary and transmitted over encrypted connections, and account-based access rules limit who can read or change it. Identity documents are kept private. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

5. Retention and deletion

Proof photos are deleted 7 days after delivery. Your other data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your account and data inside the app or on the BuyNow Rider account deletion page. Some delivery, cash and complaint records must be kept longer for accounting or legal reasons; that page explains which.

6. Your choices

  • Edit your profile in the app, and replace your document photos
  • Go offline to stop saving your live location, or turn location off in your device settings
  • Turn off notifications in your device settings
  • Ask for your account and data to be deleted
  • Contact us with any privacy question

7. Who can use the app

BuyNow Rider is for adults (18 and over) who deliver for a living. It is not directed to children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top changes when we do.
